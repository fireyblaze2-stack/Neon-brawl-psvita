Tip: rotate your phone sideways for a bigger screen.

P1: Arrows move, Z punch (chain 3 for a knockdown), X jump, Z in air = kick. P2: WASD, F punch, G jump. Press F to join as P2 any time. VS mode: press V on the title screen (Select on a pad) for a best-of-3 duel with weapons dropping into the arena. Coins earn cash for the shop between waves. CUSTOMIZE (title screen) sets your name and skin. DEFENSE MODE: build traps with 1-4 (P2: 7 8 9 0) or tap the bar at the bottom, ENTER starts the wave. On a pad: right stick picks a trap, Select places it, L3 (stick click) begins the wave. Pause menu: Esc / P, or Start on a pad. Weapons go into 3 slots (1 melee, 2 ranged): press C (P1) or H (P2) to swap, LB / LT on a pad (forward / back), or the SWAP button on touch. SWORD+SHIELD (melee slot) automatically blocks hits from the front: blocked hits do about 70% less damage and never knock you down, but each block uses up durability.
Controller: stick or D-pad to move, X (Square) or B to punch, A (Cross) to jump, Start to begin. A second controller is P2. Press any button once so the browser detects it.
